## What Is AI Tool Rate Check?

AI Tool Rate Check is a standalone pricing analysis tool that runs in a web browser. It is intended for freelancers and no-code users who need to understand how AI tools may affect their business rates. The four-tier approach helps users determine whether a tool improves their position, reduces perceived value, or makes sense as part of a retainer service.

There is no account system, backend, or tracking in the tool. That makes it practical to share, embed, or customize as a white-label asset. English and Japanese interfaces are included for use across different audiences and client workflows.
